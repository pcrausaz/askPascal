### Details 
**Ingredients**
- 1 or 2 sheets of store bough puff pastry
- 2 egg yolk
- 1 egg
- 100 g of sugar
- 45g of corn starch
- 45 cl of milk
- 1 vanilla bean
- 15 cl of heavy cream

**Method**
1. In a pan, wet the pan, do not dry it out (this helps preventing the milk from sticking)
2. Add the milk, and half of the sugar (50g)
3. Scrape and add the vanilla bean to the milk
4. Bring to a boil and let it rest for about 30 mn
5. In your mixer, add the yolk, the egg, the sugar and the corn starch
6. Blanch (whisk )
7. Pour t
