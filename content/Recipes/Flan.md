### Details 
**Ingredients**
- 1 or 2 sheets of store bough puff pastry
- 2 egg yolk
- 1 egg
- 100 g of sugar
- 45g of corn starch
- 45 cl of milk
- 1 vanilla bean
- 15 cl of heavy cream

**Method**
1. Preheat the oven at 180 °C / 350° F
2. I use a 18 cm (7 in) non-stick springform cake pan
3. Butter the pan, layout the puff pastry on the bottom and side
4. In a pan, wet the pan, do not dry it out (this helps preventing the milk from sticking)
5. Add the milk, and half of the sugar (50g)
6. Scrape and add the vanilla bean to the milk
7. Bring to a boil and let it rest for about 30 mn
8. In your mixer, add the yolk, the egg, the sugar and the corn starch
9. Blanch (whisk) the mixture
10. Re-heat the milk slightly, pour over the egg mixture, mix well
11. Place the mix into the pan, bring back to boil while mixing well
12. Let it boil a few minutes
13. Bring back the cream to the mixer, add the cream and mix well
14.
